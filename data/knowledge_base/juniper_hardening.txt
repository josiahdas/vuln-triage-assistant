vendor: juniper
product: Junos OS
topic: general hardening and patch management
---

Juniper Junos OS devices are commonly deployed as core and edge routers.
Hardening guidance:

1. Restrict J-Web (web management interface) access to trusted management
   networks; several Junos CVEs have targeted J-Web specifically, including
   authentication-bypass and remote code execution chains.
2. Apply Junos security advisories promptly, particularly for any CVE
   affecting J-Web or the management daemon (mgd), which have historically
   been the highest-severity Junos vulnerability class.
3. Use RADIUS or TACACS+ centralized authentication with role-based access
   rather than shared local accounts for administrative access.
4. Disable unused daemons and services (e.g. FTP, Telnet) via the Junos
   configuration, keeping only required management protocols enabled.
5. For internal-only devices with no direct internet exposure, patching
   urgency can reasonably be lower than for edge-facing Junos devices,
   but should still follow a defined patch cadence rather than being
   deprioritized indefinitely.
6. Enable and regularly review system logging for authentication attempts
   and configuration changes.
